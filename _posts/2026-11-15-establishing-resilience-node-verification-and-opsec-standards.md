**Resilience Node** is an independent research initiative focused on reproducible Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT), infrastructure analysis, and practical digital resilience.



### Core Research Domains



* **Open-Source Attribution:** Mapping digital infrastructure, domain histories, and network relationships using strictly public records.

* **Operational Security (OPSEC):** Hardening personal and organizational attack surfaces against passive reconnaissance and metadata leaks.

* **Reproducible Workflows:** Documenting step-by-step verification pipelines so independent analysts can audit and replicate every finding.



### Verification Protocol



Every publication on this platform adheres to a strict evidence-first standard:



1. **Primary Source Archiving:** All web artifacts, DNS records, and public filings are preserved with timestamps before analysis.

2. **Multi-Point Corroboration:** Single-source claims are never treated as confirmed facts; attribution requires independent technical signals.

3. **Clear Confidence Boundaries:** Verified technical telemetry is explicitly separated from analytical hypotheses.
