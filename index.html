Research Focus

Open-Source Intelligence

Structured collection, cross-referencing, and attribution using strictly public data sources.

Digital Resilience

Operational security (OPSEC), infrastructure hardening, and threat surface minimization.

Reproducible Workflows

Transparent analytical pipelines, verification checklists, and open documentation.

Methodology & Ethics

All publications follow a strict evidence-first standard: primary source archiving, multi-point verification, clear separation between confirmed facts and analytical hypotheses, and zero use of non-public or compromised datasets.
