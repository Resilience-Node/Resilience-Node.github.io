Capabilities

Strategic Intelligence

Structured collection and assessment of publicly available information to develop a clearer picture of relevant developments, actors, and environments.

Digital Risk Analysis

Assessment of digital exposure, technology dependencies, and infrastructure risks that may affect organisational resilience.

Research & Verification

Transparent research methods, source evaluation, and verification standards designed to make findings traceable and defensible.

Our Approach

Our work is grounded in an evidence-first approach: primary-source research, corroboration across independent sources, and a clear distinction between established facts, analytical judgement, and uncertainty. We work with publicly available information and do not rely on non-public or compromised datasets.
